# Assets of the photoreal New York and their licences

No textures, images, HDRIs or models were added. Every material, the sky, the clouds and the water are
procedural shaders (src/city/real/). Nothing was generated by an AI model. The only new files that ship are
data derived from OpenStreetMap through Overture Maps, built by this repository's own scripts.

## Data files (shipped, public/models/)

| File | What it is | Made by | Source and licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| new-york-cover.png | Parks, gardens, pitches, cemeteries, grass (r) and trees (g) on the plinth, in the ground map's frame | scripts/models/cover.py, cover.mjs | Overture Maps release 2026-10-21.0, `base/land_use` and `base/land`; © OpenStreetMap contributors and Overture Maps Foundation, ODbL |
| new-york-context.png | The 24 x 28 km box round the plinth: water (r), buildings' cover (g), parks (b), buildings' height (a) | scripts/models/context.py, context.mjs | Overture Maps 2026-10-21.0, `base/water`, `base/land_use`, `buildings/building`; © OpenStreetMap contributors and Overture Maps Foundation, ODbL |
| new-york-context.bin | 4,205 buildings of 30 m and more outside the plinth, as boxes on their footprints' principal axes | scripts/models/context.mjs | as above |
| new-york-context.json | The box's extent and the build's counts | scripts/models/context.mjs | as above |

The credit on screen already names OpenStreetMap contributors and Overture Maps (ODbL), which covers these.
Heights: the mapped height, else floors x 3.2 m, else the median of the box's known heights (8.1 m; 28,062
of 661,921 buildings took it, as the build prints).
