# Assets of the photoreal New York and Paris and their licences

No textures, images, HDRIs or models were added, for either city. Every material, the sky, the clouds and
the water are procedural shaders (src/city/real/; Paris's own in src/city/real/paris.js). Nothing was generated by an AI model. The only new files that ship are
data derived from OpenStreetMap through Overture Maps, built by this repository's own scripts.

The credit on screen already names OpenStreetMap contributors and Overture Maps (ODbL), which covers these.
Paris's heights: of 697,306 buildings in the box, 518,471 had no mapped height or floor count and took the
median known height of their 1 km cell (cells with at least 25 mapped), 2,100 the box's median (7.1 m). The
rule changed for this (scripts/models/context.mjs): the box's median is the suburbs' houses, and most
unmapped buildings stand in the six-storey city. New York's shipped files were built with the box's median
alone; rebuilding them would now use the cell's.
Heights: the mapped height, else floors x 3.2 m, else the median of the box's known heights (8.1 m; 28,062
of 661,921 buildings took it, as the build prints).
